# Sources for the 21 November 2026 stories

Each tile of `entry.png` is one story that happened, or was first reported, on Saturday
21 November 2026. The day's events were read before the poster was drawn; the program itself
uses no network.

- **Gaza — aid truck hit, 4 killed.** Gaza's civil defence agency reported four people killed by
  Israeli fire: three in a drone strike on a food-carrying vehicle in Gaza City, one by gunfire in
  Jabalia. Israel said it targeted operatives. (AFP via France 24, Reuters, Al Jazeera, 21 Nov 2026)
- **Riyadh — 3 Houthi missiles intercepted.** Saudi Arabia intercepted three missiles fired at
  Riyadh by the Houthis. (Al Jazeera, 21 Nov 2026)
- **Gulf of Finland — drone hits Russian cargo ship.** A drone struck a Russian cargo vessel in
  international waters of the Gulf of Finland and set it on fire; Finland's defence ministry then
  imposed temporary airspace and maritime restrictions. (AFP, 21 Nov 2026)
- **Bangladesh — 80 factories shut by wage strike.** At least 80 garment factories in Savar and
  Ashulia declared general holidays after sit-ins and work stoppages over minimum-wage demands;
  police said 96 people had been arrested, and the Border Guard deployed 90 platoons across seven
  districts. (The Business Standard, The Daily Star, Dhaka Tribune, 21 Nov 2026)
- **EU – Indonesia — trade deal signed.** Indonesia and the European Union signed trade and
  investment agreements lifting 98.5% of tariffs on EU goods, after a decade of talks. (Euronews,
  21 Nov 2026)
- **Mercury — BepiColombo enters orbit.** The European Space Agency announced that the BepiColombo
  spacecraft had inserted itself into orbit around Mercury. (AP, 21 Nov 2026)
- **Also on the day.** A fire at a power station in Basra, Iraq, killed at least four people and
  injured 35 (Reuters). Japan's trade ministry said Saudi Arabia would triple the crude oil it
  stores in Japan, to 24 million barrels (Japan Today).
